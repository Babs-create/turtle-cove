Crawl to the surf.

Grandmother Tide · oldest turtle on this coast

Green sea turtle · Nesting beach, 11:40 pm

Turtle CoveSunken Secrets

You just dug out of the nest. Crawl to the surf, dive to the seagrass on the sea floor, and survive as long as you can. Sand is slow going, and everything in the ocean is hungry. There is no finish line.

Move
Arrow keys or WASD. On a phone, drag anywhere on the screen.
Dash
Space, Shift or the Dash button. A quick burst of speed every few seconds. Underwater it costs a little air. Dash straight up at the surface to leap out of the water.
Dodge
A red ring marks where a gull will dive. Get out of it, or dive under the water.
Eat
Seagrass on the bottom, small fish (chase them down) and jellyfish. Bite a jellyfish's bell; its tentacles sting. Plastic bags look like jellyfish. Eating one hurts.
Hide
Leafy seagrass and the Kelp Forest make you hard for predators to spot.
Explore
Your home Atlantic has ten zones, from the Seagrass Shelf to the Kelp Forest and the Abyss. Each new zone you find is worth points.
Migrate
Swim off the east edge of the map to travel to another ocean: the Sargasso Sea, a Pacific Coral Reef, or the Frozen North (adults only). Each has its own creatures.
Nest
As an adult, crawl up your home beach and lay eggs. Guard your hatchlings from gulls as they race to the sea.
Protect
Hatchlings that reach the water follow you. Predators hunt them, so keep them close, lead them into seagrass or kelp, and dash into attackers to drive them off. Each one that stays with you long enough swims off on its own. If you die, you can carry on as one of your hatchlings.
Ride
A fast ocean current flows east past the Far Banks. Swim into the streaks to ride it.
Secrets
Every ocean hides a secret with a permanent reward, and there's one more if you're quick. Look for clues: a bottle, a glowing crack, a column of bubbles. Secrets you find stay found, even when you start over.
Treasure
Rare sunken chests glint gold on the sea floor, mostly in deep water. Giant clams hold pearls. Grab one while the shell is open.
Nets
Old ghost nets drift in the water and are hard to see until you're close. If you get tangled, wiggle in every direction to tear free.
Breathe
Air drains underwater. Touch the surface to refill. The deep zones are too deep for a hatchling's lungs.
Grow
8 bites of food makes you a juvenile. 22 makes you an adult. Then keep going.
Goals
Follow the goal at the top of the screen for bonus points. The run ends when you're eaten.
